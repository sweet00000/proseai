✏️ Your response

"I think it is basically true that the report kind of shows that customers are dissatisfied with the product, and there are a lot of areas where we can improve the experience."

🤖 AI Feedback
Cut the hedges. "I think," "basically," and "kind of" signal uncertainty. You have data — own it. Try: "The report confirms customers are dissatisfied. Three areas need immediate attention." 40% shorter, twice as confident. ✓ "dissatisfied with" and "we can improve" were strong — keep those.
